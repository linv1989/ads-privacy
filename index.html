2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Application only. It does not cover Amazon's own handling of data, which is governed by the Amazon Privacy Notice and the Amazon Ads API License Agreement and Data Protection Policy that we accepted when our API access was approved.

3. What the Application accesses

After an authorised user grants consent through Login with Amazon, the Application may access the following data through the Amazon Ads API, limited to advertising accounts that user is permitted to manage:

  • Advertising credentials and identifiers — Login with Amazon tokens (access and refresh tokens), the client ID of our Login with Amazon security profile, and the advertising profile IDs assigned to our accounts.
  • Campaign configuration — campaigns, ad groups, keywords, product ads, targeting settings, bids, budgets, negative keywords and statuses.
  • Performance and reporting data — impressions, clicks, cost, attributed sales and units, search terms and other metrics returned by the Amazon Ads reporting API.
  • Advertising account metadata — marketplace, currency, time zone and account name.

We do not access buyer personal data, customer names, shipping addresses, payment details, order-level customer records, or any data from Amazon marketplaces beyond the advertising scope granted above.

4. How we use the data

  • Generate internal performance reports (spend, ACOS, ROAS, sales, search-term analysis).
  • Adjust bids, budgets and campaign structure to improve advertising efficiency.
  • Detect anomalies such as budget exhaustion, out-of-stock advertising or runaway spend.
  • Maintain a local historical record of advertising performance, because the Amazon Ads API only retains a limited lookback window.

We do not use the data for profiling individuals, and we do not make automated decisions that affect any individual's rights.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade data obtained through the Application. Data is shared only with:

  • Amazon, as the API provider, in the ordinary course of making API requests;
  • Infrastructure providers that host our own servers or storage, under confidentiality obligations and only to the extent needed to operate the Application;
  • Authorities, where required by applicable law.

The Application is for internal use. It is not distributed to third parties and does not serve third-party advertisers.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • Credentials are stored on access-controlled infrastructure. Refresh tokens are kept in files readable only by the operating-system account running the Application (file mode 0600).
  • All traffic to Amazon endpoints uses HTTPS. We do not transmit credentials over unencrypted channels.
  • Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed automatically; refresh tokens are rotated or re-consented on expiry.
  • Advertising performance data is retained for up to 24 months for year-over-year comparison, then deleted or aggregated. Credentials are deleted when they are no longer needed or upon revocation of consent.

7. Your choices and rights

Consent can be revoked at any time in your Amazon account's permissions settings ("Manage Login with Amazon"). Once consent is revoked, the Application can no longer access your advertising data. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of data we hold, and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The Application is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Application or in applicable law. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised, and material changes will be noted on this page.
